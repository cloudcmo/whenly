Add Whenly to your home screen

It takes about 10 seconds and means Whenly is one tap away, every day. No app store needed.

1
Open whenly.co.uk in your browser
Visit whenly.co.uk in your phone's browser.
2
Tap the Share button
Look for the ⎙ icon at the bottom of the screen. It is a box with an arrow pointing up. Tap it.
3
Choose "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the menu and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add in the top right. Done!
1
Open Whenly in Chrome
Make sure you're using Chrome. It works best for adding to your home screen on Android.
2
Tap the three-dot menu
Look for the ⋮ icon in the top right corner of Chrome. Tap it to open the menu.
3
Choose "Add to Home screen"
Tap Add to Home screen from the menu, then tap Add. Whenly will appear on your home screen like an app.
🎯
That's it!
Whenly will sit on your home screen. Tap it every day for a fresh set of years to guess.
